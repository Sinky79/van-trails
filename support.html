Support

Last updated 10 October 2026

Something not working, or an idea for the road? Email support@ferryislandsoftware.com. Please include your device model, its iOS version and what happened.

Common questions

How do I drive? Hold GO to drive and BRAKE to slow down; the arrows steer. The gold marker shows where to go next.

Where is my progress saved? On your device, automatically, after everything that changes it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Where are my pictures? In the game's Trip Diary, with their captions. To keep one outside the game, tap SHARE on the picture: send it anywhere iOS offers, or choose Save Image for your photo library (iOS asks your permission the first time).

The game feels slow or warm. Close other apps and let the device cool down; if it continues, email us your device model.

Testing with TestFlight

If you are testing a beta, send feedback from the TestFlight app (take a screenshot in the game and tap Share Beta Feedback), or email us.
