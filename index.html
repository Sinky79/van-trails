A road trip game for iPhone and iPad

Van Trails

The van is yours, the coast road is open, and the dream is an invitation to the camp-out on the mountain top.

Drive a hand-painted island in your orange and cream van: take jobs in the villages, camp under the stars, fit out the van in the Workshop, and photograph the road. Post your best pictures, win followers, and one day the postcard comes.
